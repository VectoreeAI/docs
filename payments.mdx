People who use your agent pay **you**, and that money comes back to you. This is how the app becomes a product you can charge for — powered by Stripe.

It sits after [Deploy](/deploy) on the same project: a public app, then a way to get paid.

That is a different flow from the organization wallet on [Billing](/billing):

| | Organization wallet | Payments |
|---|---|---|
| Status | Live today | Coming soon |
| Who pays | You pay Vectoree | Your users pay you |
| For | Model and Tool Hub usage | Using the app you shipped |
